# Great Courses

A curated directory of standout university lecture series, specialized MOOCs, and comprehensive audiovisual courses exploring specialized traditions, formal logic, epic hermeneutics, and modern developments in Indian philosophy.

## Epistemology, Logic, and Systematic Darśana

- [Classical Indian Philosophy (History of Philosophy Without Any Gaps / King's College London & LMU Munich / Peter Adamson & Jonardon Ganeri)](https://historyofphilosophy.net/series/classical-indian-philosophy)
- [Symbolic Logic and Formal Reasoning (NPTEL / IIT Kharagpur / Prof. Chhanda Chakraborti)](https://nptel.ac.in)
- [Philosophy and Critical Thinking (NPTEL / IIT-ISM Dhanbad)](https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc24_hs75/preview)

## Buddhist, Jaina, and Heterodox Traditions

- [Buddhism Through Its Scriptures (HarvardX / Harvard University / Prof. Charles Hallisey)](https://www.edx.org/learn/religion/harvard-university-buddhism-through-its-scriptures)
- [Buddhist Philosophy (SWAYAM / Central Institute of Higher Tibetan Studies / Prof. Pradeep Prabhakar Gokhale)](https://onlinecourses.swayam2.ac.in)
- [Buddhism and Modern Psychology (Coursera / Princeton University / Prof. Robert Wright)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/science-of-meditation)

## Vedānta, Yoga, and Tantric Metaphysics

- [Vedānta & Samkhya (Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies / OCHS Online)](https://ochsonline.org)
- [Philosophy of Yoga: Theory, Text, and Practice (Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies / OCHS Online)](https://ochsonline.org/course/philosophy-of-yoga/)
- [Tantra: History, Text, and Philosophy (Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies / Prof. Gavin Flood)](https://ochsonline.org)

## Epic Hermeneutics, Ethics, and Modern Indian Thought

- [Mahabharata: India's Epic and Moral Philosophy (Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies / Dr. Nick Sutton & Dr. Raj Balkaran)](https://ochsonline.org)
- [Bhagavad Gita: Textual and Philosophical Explorations (Oxford Centre for Hindu Studies / OCHS Online)](https://ochsonline.org/course/bhagavad-gita/)
- [Contemporary Indian Philosophy (NPTEL / IIT Bombay / Dr. Ratikanta Panda)](https://nptel.ac.in)
