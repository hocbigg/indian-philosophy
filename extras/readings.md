# Great Readings

A curated collection of field-defining primary texts, groundbreaking monographs, and seminal philosophical investigations in Indian philosophy. These works demonstrate how classical and modern thinkers formulated paradigms, constructed formal logical systems, and challenged dogmatic orthodoxy.

## Foundational Primary Classics and Landmark Commentaries

| Title | Author(s) | Type | Why It's Worth Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [*The Principal Upaniṣads*](https://archive.org/details/the-principal-upanishads-by-dr.-s-radhakrishnan) | S. Radhakrishnan (Ed. & Trans.) | Primary Text Edition | The foundational textual sourcebook containing the thirteen major Vedic-Upaniṣadic dialogues on the nature of Brahman, Ātman, and ultimate reality. |
| [*Mūlamadhyamakakārikā (The Fundamental Wisdom of the Middle Way)*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195093360) | Nāgārjuna (Trans. Jay L. Garfield) | Primary Text & Commentary | The foundational treatise of Madhyamaka Buddhism, presenting an uncompromising dialectical deconstruction of intrinsic nature (svabhāva) through dependent origination. |
| [*The Nyāya-Sūtras of Gautama with the Commentary of Vātsyāyana*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9788120812642) | Gautama & Vātsyāyana (Trans. Ganganatha Jha) | Classical Commentary | The seminal treatise establishing Indian realism, five-stage inferential syllogisms, epistemology (pramāṇa), and formal rules of disputation (vāda). |
| [*The Brahma Sūtra Śaṅkara Bhāṣya*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9788175051058) | Śaṅkarācārya (Trans. Swami Gambhirananda) | Classical Commentary | The crowning masterpiece of Advaita Vedānta, constructing a rigorous non-dualist metaphysics that reconciles Upaniṣadic revelation with rational exegesis. |

## Epistemology, Logic, and Philosophy of Language

| Title | Author(s) | Type | Why It's Worth Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [*Perception: An Essay on Classical Indian Theories of Knowledge*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780198246251) | Bimal Krishna Matilal | Monograph | A landmark philosophical study comparing classical Indian debates on perception, direct realism, and phenomenalism with contemporary analytic epistemology. |
| [*The Character of Logic in India*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780791437407) | Bimal Krishna Matilal (Eds. Jonardon Ganeri & Heeraman Tiwari) | Monograph | The definitive reassessment of Indian logic, demonstrating how Nyāya and Buddhist inference operate as epistemically grounded logics of rational support rather than formal tautologies. |

Dreyfus | Monograph | A comprehensive, rigorous investigation of Dharmakīrti's epistemological turn, nominalism, perception, and causal efficacy (arthakriyā). |
| [*The Word and the World: India's Contribution to the Study of Language*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195625158) | Bimal Krishna Matilal | Monograph | The essential exploration of Sanskrit linguistic philosophy, analyzing Bhartṛhari’s sentence-holism (sphoṭa), Mīmāṃsā compositionality, and Buddhist negative semantics (apoha). |

## Metaphysics, Consciousness, and Non-Dualisms

| Title | Author(s) | Type | Why It's Worth Reading |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [*Nāgārjuna's Metaphysics: A Philosophical Investigation*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780195384963) | Jan Westerhoff | Monograph | An analytical reconstruction of Nāgārjuna's anti-foundationalism, systematically examining emptiness across physical objects, causality, motion, and the self. |
| [*The Concealed Art of the Soul: Theories of Self and Practices of Truth in Indian Ethics and Epistemology*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780199202416) | Jonardon Ganeri | Monograph | A transformative study exploring first-person subjectivity, introspection, and the classical debates between substantialist ātman and Buddhist anātman. |
| [*Buddhist Phenomenology: A Philosophical Investigation of Yogācāra Buddhism*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780415406109) | Dan Lusthaus | Monograph | A groundbreaking reassessment arguing that Yogācāra is not metaphysical idealism, but an advanced phenomenological critique of cognitive reification and intentionality. |
| [*The Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā of Utpaladeva with the Author's Vṛtti*](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9788120818675) | Utpaladeva (Trans. Raffaele Torella) | Primary Text & Critical Edition | The foundational text of the Kashmir Pratyabhijñā school, articulating a dynamic non-dualism where consciousness is both self-luminous (prakāśa) and self-reflective (vimarśa). |

## Comparative, Analytic, and Decolonial Reconstructions

| Title | Author(s) | Type | Why It's Worth Reading |
