# Projects

A curated collection of step-by-step methodological guides, computational toolkits, open templates, and analytical workflows for practicing Indian philosophy through hands-on research and artifact creation.

## Textual Hermeneutics, Translation, and Critical Commentary

- [Build a Critical Sanskrit Commentary (Bhāṣya and Ṭīkā) Apparatus](https://tei-c.org/guidelines/p5/) – Guide to structuring line-by-line philosophical exegesis, word-ordering (anvaya), and critical apparatus according to classical hermeneutical standards.
- [Philological Translation and Collation of Indic Manuscripts Workflow (SARIT)](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus) – Step-by-step guide for transcribing, collating, and critically editing philosophical manuscripts with variant readings.
- [Muktabodha Sanskrit E-Text and Manuscript Archive Research Workflow](https://muktabodha.org) – Methodological toolkit for searching, cross-referencing, and analyzing unedited primary sources in non-dual Śaiva, Tantric, and early philosophical corpora.
- [GRETIL Electronic Text Processing and Concordance Building Guide](http://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil.html) – Workflow for generating textual concordances, cross-textual citation indexes, and parallel phrase locators from canonical Indic corpora.

## Formal Logic, Nyāya Syllogistics, and Argument Mapping

- [Construct a 5-Step Nyāya Syllogism (Pañcāvayava) Argument Map](https://iep.utm.edu/nyaya/) – Practical methodology for diagramming classical Indian arguments from proposition (pratijñā) to conclusion (nigamana).
- [Navya-Nyāya Logic Formalization and Invariable Concomitance (Vyāpti) Analysis](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/analytic-india/) – Step-by-step workflow for formalizing inferential conditions, eliminating logical ambiguity, and mapping absences (abhāva).
- [Indian Epistemic Fallacy Checker (Hetvābhāsa Diagnostic Framework)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-india/) – Analytical template for testing, classifying, and refuting material and formal fallacies in Indian debates.
- [Buddhist Inferential Proof Modeling (Trairūpya-Hetu Validation Guide)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dharmakirti/) – Workflow for formalizing Dignāga and Dharmakīrti's three conditions of valid reason and nominalist inference.

## Dialectical Modeling, Debate Rules, and Multi-Valued Systems

- [Design a Classical Indian Philosophical Debate Tree (Vāda-Kathā Protocol)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-india/) – Methodological guide for structuring authentic disputations using classical rules of honest inquiry (vāda), disputation (jalpa), and cavil (vitaṇḍā).
- [Build a Jaina Seven-Valued Truth-Table (Syādvāda and Saptabhaṅgī Modeler)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/jaina-philosophy/) – Procedural guide for calculating multi-perspective modal judgments across the seven permutations of predication.
- [Madhyamaka Tetralemma (Catuṣkoṭi) Dialectical Matrix](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/twotruths-india/) – Step-by-step guide for constructing four-cornered dialectical refutations of inherent existence (svabhāva).
- [Indian Theories of Perceptual Illusion (Khyātivāda) Comparison Matrix](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/perception-india/) – Analytical framework for modeling rival Indian theories of error and intentionality across Advaita, Nyāya, Mīmāṃsā, and Buddhism.

## Computational Indology, Corpus Linguistics, and Digital Text Encoding

- [Parse and Disambiguate Complex Sanskrit Philosophy Sentences with the INRIA Heritage Platform](https://sanskrit.inria.fr) – Step-by-step guide to using the Gérard Huet Sanskrit Heritage engine for morphological tagging, sandhi resolution, and compound analysis.
- [Corpus-Driven Epistemic Terminology Extraction using the Digital Corpus of Sanskrit (DCS)](http://www.sanskrit-linguistics.org/dcs/) – Analytical workflow for querying syntactic dependency relations, frequency distributions, and historical semantic shifts in philosophical texts.
- [Encode Indic Philosophical Texts using TEI-XML Guidelines](https://github.com/sarit/SARIT-corpus) – Practical walkthrough for marking up structural hierarchy, quotations, speaker turns, intertextual references, and critical apparatus in TEI-compliant XML.
- [Build a Digital Lexicon and Concept-Ontology for Sanskrit Philosophical Systems](https://sanskrit.inria.fr) – Guide for constructing structured computational dictionaries and ontological taxonomy trees for padārthas (ontological categories).

## Cross-Cultural Conceptual Analysis and Comparative Reconstruction

- [Construct a Gettier vs. Pramāṇa Comparative Epistemic Analysis](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/epistemology-india/) – Research workflow for mapping Indian factivism and knowledge sources (pramāṇa-vāda) against contemporary Western justified-true-belief problems.
- [Translate Mind and Intentionality across Traditions (Buddhist Reflexive Awareness vs. Modern Philosophy of Mind)](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/mind-indian-buddhism/) – Step-by-step guide to conducting philosophically sound conceptual translation between Sanskrit cognitive concepts (vijñāna, svasaṃvedana, citta-vṛtti) and contemporary cognitive science.
- [Reconstruct an Indian Ethics and Agency Case Study from Epic and Legal Texts](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/ethics-indian-buddhism/) – Methodological framework for analyzing moral dilemmas (dharma-saṃkaṭa), moral luck, and intentional agency in classical narratives.
